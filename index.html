Research project

Satellite-derived bathymetry, Plage des Sablettes

A full pipeline from Google Earth Engine image processing to machine learning for shallow-water depth mapping. Stumpf and Lyzenga ratio models are compared with Random Forest regression and validated with spatial block cross-validation.

Delivered QGIS maps, an interactive topo-bathymetry viewer and a 19-page report.

Sentinel-2 · Google Earth Engine · Python (scikit-learn) · QGIS
