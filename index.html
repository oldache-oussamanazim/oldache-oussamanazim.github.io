About Me

I hold a Master's degree in Marine Geosciences and Coastal Engineering from USTHB (Algiers), built on a Licence in Applied Geology.

My work combines field surveying with satellite remote sensing, geospatial analysis and numerical modelling. I focus on satellite-derived shallow-water bathymetry, shoreline change, sediment dynamics, marine water quality and coastal infrastructure monitoring.

My toolkit spans Sentinel-2 processing (ESA SNAP, ACOLITE, Google Earth Engine), GIS (ArcGIS Pro, QGIS), Python workflows (rasterio, scikit-learn) and wave modelling (SWAN).

I am seeking funded academic opportunities and professional roles in marine geosciences, coastal engineering, hydrography and remote sensing.

Projects

Research Project

Satellite-Derived Bathymetry: Plage des Sablettes, Algiers

Full pipeline from Google Earth Engine image processing to machine learning for shallow-water depth mapping. Compares empirical ratio models (Stumpf, Lyzenga) with Random Forest regression, validated with spatial block cross-validation. Outputs include QGIS products and an interactive topo-bathymetry HTML viewer.

Result: three-model comparison on a real beach site, documented in a 19-page report.

Tools: Sentinel-2, Google Earth Engine, Python (scikit-learn), QGIS

GitHub repository
Master's Thesis

Marine Pollution: Bay of Algiers

Coupled in situ physico-chemical measurements with Sentinel-2 optical indices to map water quality parameters and the spatial distribution of pollution across the Bay of Algiers. Defended June 2026 with mention Très Bien.

Result: calibrated satellite-based water quality maps of the bay.

Tools: Sentinel-2, SNAP, Google Earth Engine, Python, GIS

GitHub repository
Master's Project

Shoreline Change Analysis

Shoreline extraction from Sentinel-2 imagery and erosion/accretion analysis with DSAS in ArcGIS Pro along the coast from Sidi Fredj to Douaouda, west of Algiers.

Tools: Sentinel-2, ArcGIS Pro, DSAS

Master's Project

Wave Modelling with SWAN

Wave and swell modelling for several zones of the Algerian coast, using GMRT bathymetry, Copernicus Marine wave forcing and NEMO currents. Outputs: wave height, period, direction and nearshore refraction maps.

Tools: SWAN, Copernicus Marine, GIS, Python

GitHub repository
Field Campaign

Tigzirt Coastal Assessment

On-site survey with a Topcon GM-52 total station, multi-temporal shoreline tracking and evaluation of coastal protection and gabion structures.

Tools: Topcon GM-52, AutoCAD, Google Earth, QGIS

GitHub repository
