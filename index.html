About Me

I am a graduate in Marine Geosciences and Coastal Engineering from USTHB, with a background in Applied Geology.


My academic and project experience focuses on coastal and marine environments, with particular interest in coastal processes, sediment dynamics, bathymetry, marine environmental assessment and geospatial analysis.


I have worked with field measurements, satellite imagery, GIS and numerical modelling to analyse marine and coastal systems. My technical work includes Sentinel-2 remote sensing, Google Earth Engine, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Python, MATLAB, SWAN and Delft3D.


I am currently looking for professional opportunities where I can apply and develop my skills in marine geosciences, coastal engineering, GIS, remote sensing and environmental analysis.
